# VISION

> **Nosce te ipsum.**
> *Know thyself.*

For thousands of years, humanity has searched for ways to understand itself.

Books, philosophy, psychology, education, and reflection have all offered pieces of the answer. Today, digital games capture something none of them could before: millions of authentic decisions made under challenge, curiosity, cooperation, persistence, and exploration.

Psukhe exists to transform those decisions into meaningful self-knowledge.

---

## Vision

To help people know themselves through play.

We envision a future where gameplay becomes a trusted source of self-discovery—revealing how people learn, solve problems, adapt, collaborate, and grow.

Self-knowledge is not the destination; it is the foundation.

By understanding ourselves better, we can make better decisions, pursue more fulfilling careers, build stronger teams, and connect with people who genuinely think and play like us.

---

## Mission

Build the most transparent and explainable framework for understanding human behavior through gameplay.

---

## Principles

### Explainability First

Every insight must be supported by observable evidence.

People deserve to understand why a conclusion exists—not simply receive a score.

---

### Evidence over Assumptions

Behavioral insights emerge from measurable actions rather than stereotypes or speculation.

---

### Scientific Integrity

Our models evolve through research, validation, and reproducible methods.

---

### User Ownership

Players own their data.

Psukhe exists to help users understand themselves—not to exploit their attention or their information.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Understanding yourself is only the beginning.

As Psukhe grows, it can become a place where people discover opportunities, careers, collaborators, creators, teammates, and communities that genuinely align with how they think, learn, and play.

The first step is self-discovery.

Everything else grows from there.

---

## Our Goal

Help people know themselves.

Everything else begins there.
