# Psukhe - What if your gaming history could tell a story about how you think, learn, and solve problems?

> Understand behavior through play.

Psukhe is an open-source research initiative exploring whether gameplay can reveal meaningful, explainable patterns of human behavior.

Rather than relying on questionnaires or self-reported personality tests, Psukhe studies what players actually do inside games. Every achievement, challenge, exploration, and decision contributes to a behavioral profile grounded in observable actions.

---

## Vision

Games are one of the richest behavioral datasets ever created.

Millions of players voluntarily solve problems, explore worlds, cooperate, compete, and make thousands of meaningful decisions inside carefully designed systems. Those actions form behavioral patterns that deserve to be studied with the same rigor applied to other forms of behavioral data.

Our mission is to transform gameplay into explainable behavioral intelligence.
